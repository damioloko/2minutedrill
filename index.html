How the season is built

Two divisions of five. You play each division rival twice and everyone in the other division once — then one extra game against the team that finished in your spot in the other division last year. That’s 14 games. This year the rivalry rematch lands in Week 14.

1 · Champ picks their opponent

From 2026, the regular-season champion chooses who they face in the semi-final.

2 · Divisions

Two divisions of five. Play divisional members twice, the opposite division once, plus a rematch with your same-placed rival from last year. Playoffs stay at five teams: each division’s top seed gets a bye; the two second seeds and the Most PF wildcard enter the all-play.

3 · Draft order by Max PF

From 2026, next year’s draft order is set by Max PF for every team except the two finalists.
